## 📞 AI Call Assistant

### Your Phone, Even When You Can't Pick Up. 🤖

**Busy? In a meeting? At work? Can't touch your phone? Let your AI handle it.**

This AI acts as a **personal call receptionist** that answers incoming calls when you're unavailable, understands why the caller is calling, follows your rules, and gives you a complete summary afterward.

---

### 🧠 How It Works

**📞 Call comes in → 🤖 AI answers → 🧠 Understands → ⚡ Takes action → 📝 Summarizes → 📊 Reports**

---

### ✨ What It Can Do

- 🚨 **Spam?** Identify it and block or reject it based on your rules.
- 💼 **Recruiter calling?** Politely collect the company, role, interview details, and callback information.
- 👨‍💻 **Work call?** Understand the purpose and record the important details.
- 📦 **Delivery call?** Handle simple questions without disturbing you.
- ⭐ **Important caller?** Continue the conversation and notify you.
- 📝 **Every call?** Get a summary of what they asked and what the AI answered.

---

### 🎯 Smart Rules

Tell the AI how you want your calls handled:

> *"During work hours, screen unknown calls. If it's a recruiter, mark it as important and collect the interview details."*

The assistant follows **your preferences** instead of treating every call the same.

---

### 📊 After Every Call

**Caller:** ABC Technologies  
**Purpose:** Interview opportunity  
**They asked:** Availability for a technical interview  
**AI answered:** Collected the details and marked the call important  
**Status:** ⭐ Needs your attention

---

### 🔥 The Goal

**Stop missing important calls without letting every random call interrupt your day.**

## 🎙️ AI Interview Simulator

### Don't just practice interviews. **Level up through them.** 🚀

A **voice/video AI interviewer** that simulates realistic interview rounds based on your **target company, role, and skill level**.

Choose a role → enter the interview → answer naturally → get evaluated → unlock harder rounds.

---

### 🧠 How It Works

**Choose Company + Role → HR Round → Technical Round → AI Evaluation → Unlock Next Level**

The AI dynamically adapts the interview based on how you perform.

### 🎯 Adaptive Interview

**Good answer?** → Harder follow-up 🔥  
**Weak answer?** → Deeper probing 🧠  
**Struggling?** → Different question / skill check  
**Strong performance?** → 🔓 Next level unlocked

### 🎥 Choose Your Mode

**🎙️ Voice Mode** — Have a natural spoken interview with the AI.

**📹 Video Mode** — Practice a face-to-face interview experience with AI.

### 🏆 Progressive Levels

**LEVEL 1** → HR + Fundamentals  
**LEVEL 2 🔓** → Technical + Scenario Questions  
**LEVEL 3 🔓** → Advanced Technical + Follow-ups  
**LEVEL 4 🔓** → Company-Style Mock Interview

You don't simply select the next level — **you earn it through performance.**

### 🏢 Company-Specific Practice

Select a target company and role to get a simulation tailored around **publicly known interview patterns, role requirements, and company principles**.

> Example: **Amazon → SDE → HR → Technical → Leadership-Principle-Based Scenarios**

*This is a practice simulation, not an actual company interview or a reproduction of confidential interview questions.*

### 📊 Your Interview Report

After every session, AI generates:

**Technical Skills** · **Communication** · **Problem Solving** · **HR Performance**

Plus:
- 🎯 Strong areas
- ⚠️ Weak areas
- 🧩 Questions you struggled with
- 📚 Topics to practice
- 🔓 Next-level eligibility
